## Who I Am

I build things that sit between hardware and software — sensors that talk to phones, boards that get inspected by cameras, systems that automate the boring parts. Bachelor's in Computer Engineering (Embedded Systems & IoT) from ISIMA, University of Monastir. Based in Monastir, Tunisia.

Right now I split my time between production/IT systems at a manufacturing plant and personal projects in Flutter and computer vision.
